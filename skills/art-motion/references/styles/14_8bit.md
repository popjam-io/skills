# 8-bit pixel game (NES era, 1985)

> Adapted from huashu-art-motion (MIT, (c) 2026 alchaincyf) - references/风格配方/14_8bit.md

Layer: material (works on any subject) - Upstream quality: ★★★ - Known weakness: a sprite overlapped scenery; 1px black outlines make every overlap glaring.

## Look
Square pixels (1 art pixel = 8 screen px at 1920 wide), fixed palette, no anti-aliasing, a 1px black outline on every sprite part, gradients only by checker dither, single-pixel faces, a pixel-font HUD. Sprites: #000000 #fcfcfc #bcbcbc #e82704 #7c0b02 #0856ef #0838a8 #f9e0ab #e8a878 #f8d038 #c89010 #f39d48 #d85214 #f8a0b0. Sky #0856ef / #47c4f8 / #aae9fb; brick highlight #fbe2a6.

## Build it in Remotion
- A `<canvas>` at width/8 x height/8 (240x135; 135x240 vertical; 1080x1350 needs a factor dividing both, e.g. 9 -> 120x150), stretched with `imageRendering: "pixelated"`; integer `fillRect`s only.
- Background cached once: 6px cross-dot wallpaper (3x3 crosses, odd rows offset 3), planks with a black seam every 24px, bricks 16x6 + 1px mortar (odd rows offset 8, top/left edge #fbe2a6). Gradients are flat bands; within +-0.05 of a boundary pick the colour by `(x + y) % 2`.
- Sprites: string bitmaps, or vector parts drawn at 1/8 scale and "spriteized" per part: alpha < 110 -> transparent, else nearest palette colour; then transparent pixels with an opaque 4-neighbour turn black. Outline each part (far arm, body, hair, face, fringe, near arm) separately - one outline round the whole figure fuses them. Cache poses.
- Faces by hand: eyes 1x2 + brow; animal eyes 2x2 with a white top-left pixel; nose 1px pink; blush 2x1; anchors / 8, rounded. Tiny props (a 6x5 mug) drawn directly; downscaled AA vectors that small turn to mush.
- HUD at full res: Press Start 2P (@remotion/google-fonts) 48px (font pixel = 6 screen px), 8px black shadow; 64px collided. Icons as 8x8 bitmaps.
- Very cheap per frame.

## Make it move
Step in whole art pixels; never tween between them.
- Tail or limb: poses -0.55, 0, 0.55, 0 rad at `Math.floor(frame / 2) % 4`.
- HUD heart blink; foliage offsets [0, 1, 0, -1]; a ?-block bumping a coin 9 art px on a half-sine (~5 frames).
- Clouds 1 art px per 2 frames; steam as three columns of 1x2 dots rising 1px per frame, looping every 6px.
Rates suit the upstream 0.23 s shot; in longer ads step loops at 6-12 fps.

## Signature transition
Pixelate: the old shot's blocks grow 1 -> 72px (ease-in), hard cut at the midpoint, the new shot's blocks shrink 72 -> 1. Upstream 0.12 s; 8-12 frames in an ad. Canvas shots: draw into a W/bs x H/bs canvas, scale up with smoothing off. DOM shots: a custom presentation (see 16_2026.md) with an SVG pixelate filter (feFlood 2x2 sample -> feComposite to block size -> feTile -> feComposite in SourceGraphic -> feMorphology dilate block/2), block size updated per frame; verify in a preview.

## Pitfalls
- Plan placement on the art-pixel grid; outlines expose overlaps.
- getImageData is non-premultiplied; don't divide by alpha.
- Scale the pixel canvas by an integer factor or pixels go uneven.
